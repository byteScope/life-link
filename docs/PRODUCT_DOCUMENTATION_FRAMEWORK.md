## How to Use This Framework

### Step 1: Copy This Template
Create a new document for your product using this framework as a template.

### Step 2: Fill in the Sections
Go through each section and fill in the details specific to your product. Not all sections may be relevant - customize as needed.

### Step 3: Collaborate
Share with your team for review and feedback. This is a living document that should evolve.

### Step 4: Keep It Updated
Update the document as the product evolves, new information becomes available, or requirements change.

### Step 5: Use for Alignment
Use this document to:
- Align team members on product vision
- Communicate with stakeholders
- Guide development decisions
- Track progress against goals
- Make strategic decisions
